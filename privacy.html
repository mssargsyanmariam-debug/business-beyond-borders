What data we collect

  • Your country (automatically). To show prices in the right currency, the website asks the free service country.is which country your IP address belongs to. We only use the two-letter country code, keep it in your browser for the current visit, and don't store it on any server. country.is states that it does not log requests.
  • Contact form. Your name, email, optional phone/WhatsApp number and message, so we can reply.
  • Feedback form. Your name, optional role and company, rating and feedback. We only publish feedback on the website if you tick the permission box.
  • Free toolkit. Your email address, to give you access to the downloads and, only if you tick the box, to send the newsletter.
  • Newsletter. If you subscribe to The Beyond Borders Brief (sent every two weeks), we store your email address to send it. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any issue or emailing us.
  • Where form data is kept. Everything you send through the website's forms is saved in a private Google Sheet that only we can open, and we receive an email copy. Your language, country and the page you used are saved with it.
  • Payments. Card payments are processed by [Stripe] (EUR, USD) and ACBA Bank (AMD). We never see or store your full card number.
  • Membership. Your name and email address, to give you access to the community and send session invitations, plus anything you choose to share in community channels.

The site remembers your language, currency and toolkit access in your browser's local storage. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.
