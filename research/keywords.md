# Keyword research: Business Beyond Borders

*Prepared 2026-09-11. Based on 2026 industry articles, competitor communities and LinkedIn/B2B trend reports (sources at the bottom). No paid keyword tool was used, so there are **no search volumes** here. Before spending on ads, check volumes in Google Keyword Planner and track real queries in Google Search Console.*

## What the 2026 market is talking about

- **Video and LinkedIn Live** are LinkedIn's fastest-growing formats. Short vertical video with captions performs best.
- **AI is everyday business.** Most B2B marketers use AI weekly. "AI agents" and automation are the SME topic of the year.
- **Thought leadership and original insights** are what buyers trust, and AI assistants increasingly quote LinkedIn articles and newsletters. That's why **GEO** (generative engine optimization: being cited by AI search) matters.
- **Personal branding and social selling** now drive B2B leads more than company pages.
- **Curated, vetted communities** beat open networking groups. Mastermind groups are usually small peer circles.
- **Armenia and its diaspora** are building networks that bridge Armenia, Europe and the US. That's a clear niche for this brand.
- **Women founders' communities** (Ellevate, Entreprenista and others) show demand for women-focused paid networks at roughly $40/month.
